Access control

Only people whose Google email is listed in the Team Roster (and not marked Inactive) can open this dashboard. Admins, whose role contains Director, Coordinator or Co-Lead, can edit the roster, verticals and these settings. Everyone else can view data and update tasks. To remove someone's access, delete them or mark them Inactive in the roster.
